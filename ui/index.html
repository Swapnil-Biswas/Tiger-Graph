Dual-Gate Action Policy Matrix (R1–R10)

Autonomous LLMs cannot be trusted with punitive banking actions. Our deterministic policy gate intercepts every swarm proposal against 10 strict institutional risk boundaries.

R1

Weak Signal Block Barrier

Prohibits card blocks when P(Fraud) < 0.70 without customer verification inquiry.

R2

Customer Denial Protocol

Customer report of unauthorized activity mandates immediate card block and case creation.

R3

Customer Clearance Protocol

Customer confirmation of legitimate travel clears suspicion, issues ALLOW, and safely closes case.

R4

24h Non-Response Hold

Unanswered customer inquiry window enforces DECLINE on subject transaction with card monitoring.

R5

Step-Up Auth Failure

Biometric or OTP authentication challenge failure triggers immediate block and high-priority escalation.

R6

BSA Structuring Escalation

Multi-entity aggregate spend ≥ $10,000 within 24h triggers mandatory FinCEN Form 111 SAR filing.

R7

Subscription Safeguard

Recognized recurring subscription billing protects cardholders from automated false-positive blocks.

R8

High-Exposure Tier L2

Unresolved exposure ≥ $1,000 prohibits autonomous closure and requires Senior L2 Investigator sign-off.

R9

Graph Evidentiary Grounding

Actions lacking explicit multi-hop graph citation IDs are rejected and returned for re-traversal.

R10

Syndicate Block Threshold

Syndicate-wide card blocking requires ≥ 2 confirmed compromised cards sharing device nexus.
